## (c) Owner questions — where a decided row conflicts and no form keeps it as written

**(c)-1 — `<Activity>` and G1/P2's word "mount" (DU-W2).** G1 says approval cards are seeded only from a read made *since the view mounted*; P2 says run-report and written-file cards *die with the view*. Activity keeps a hidden view's state and destroys its effects. The form in W04 keeps both behaviours — a reveal stamp so a pre-hide snapshot never seeds a card, an always-mounted recorder of the daemon's word, P2's cards cleared on hide, dialogs closed on hide, one boundary per view — but it reads "mount" as "shown". What changes for a person: scroll position and DOM survive a view swap; a return to a view is not a lazy re-render. **Options:** (a) adopt M70 in that form; (b) keep the unmounting swap. **Recommendation: (a)** — G1's purpose (never draw an answered card) and P2's are kept, and the G1 suite gains a hide/reveal leg per case.

Tauri's webview-versions table gives macOS 13.3 → WebKit 615.1.26.11.22 → Safari 16.4 [Docs-V: tauri-docs `reference/webview-versions.md`] (the Tauri verifier did not find which Ventura point release bundles it — one reading against the other, settled by E5 on the candidate OS). macOS 11 and 12 reach Safari 16.4 only through a Safari update, which `LSMinimumSystemVersion` cannot express [Docs: Apple Safari 16.4 release notes]. **Options:** (a) `minimumSystemVersion` 13.3 with Vite 8's default target and `tsconfig` at its ES year; (b) 11.0 plus a stated "Safari 16.4 or later" requirement in the README and release notes; (c) a lower target — not available, Tailwind v4 needs 16.4. **Recommendation: (a)**; Q18 applies the answer.
